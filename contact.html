International Logistics FAQ

How does overseas shipping work?

We package every unit in heavy-duty maritime steel crates. Products ship FCL (Full Container Load) or LCL with complete customs paperwork directly to your nearest port or jobsite.

What is the production lead time?

Standard production takes 4 to 6 weeks following CAD drawing sign-off, followed by maritime freight typically taking 20-30 days depending on the destination port.

Are installation instructions provided?

Yes! We include 3D step-by-step CAD installation guides, video walkthroughs, and direct phone/video engineering support during your framing and glazing installation.
