OUR GLASS SOLUTIONS

Premium Glass for Every Home

From energy-efficient double glazing to decorative and toughened safety glass — discover our full range of high-quality glass options for windows and doors.

🪟

Glass Types

We offer a wide selection of glass solutions designed to improve thermal efficiency, security, privacy and aesthetics in your home.

🏠

Double Glazing

Two panes of glass with an insulating argon gas fill. Reduces heat loss, cuts energy bills and minimises external noise for a more comfortable home.

❄️

Triple Glazing

Three glass panes for maximum thermal insulation and acoustic performance. Ideal for colder climates or rooms facing busy roads.

🛡️

Toughened Safety Glass

Thermally treated to be up to 5× stronger than standard glass. Shatters into small, safe fragments — perfect for doors, low-level glazing and high-traffic areas.

🌿

Laminated Glass

A plastic interlayer bonds two glass panes together. Even if broken, the glass stays in place — providing excellent security and UV protection.

🎨

Decorative & Leaded Glass

Add a classic or contemporary touch with frosted, etched, stained or leaded glass designs. Perfect for front doors, side panels and bathroom windows.

🔒

Obscure / Privacy Glass

Provides natural light while maintaining privacy. Available in various obscure levels — ideal for bathrooms, en-suites and side-entrance doors.

☀️

Self-Cleaning Glass

A special coating breaks down organic dirt and allows rainwater to wash it away — keeping your windows cleaner for longer with minimal maintenance.

🔆

Solar Control Glass

Reduces solar heat gain while maintaining excellent light transmission. Keeps rooms cooler in summer and reduces glare — ideal for south-facing windows and conservatories.
