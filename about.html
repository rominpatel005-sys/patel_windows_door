Our Manufacturing Philosophy

Robotic CNC Precision. Master Craftsmanship.

Every profile begins with aerospace-grade 6063-T6 aluminum extrusion, cut on 5-axis German CNC milling centers with sub-millimeter tolerances. We integrate multidirectional polyamide thermal breaks from Technoform (Germany) and automated double-butyl seal lines for our insulated glass units.

Unlike regional fabricators with limited options, our vertical manufacturing ecosystem gives us total control over powder coating (AAMA 2605 compliant 30-year fluoropolymer finish), tempered & laminated glass processing, and automated hardware fitment.
